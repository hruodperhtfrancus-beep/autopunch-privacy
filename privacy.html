AutoPunch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

AutoPunch is an Android app that records the hours you spend at work by detecting when you arrive at and leave your workplace. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

LOCATION DATA

AutoPunch uses your device's location for one purpose: to detect when you enter or leave the workplace area you set, so it can clock you in and out automatically. This works in the background, including when the app is closed or not in use. The app asks for your agreement before requesting location permission.

WHAT IS STORED

  • The workplace coordinates and radius you choose.
  • The start and end time of each work session.

WHERE IT IS STORED

All of this is stored only on your device. AutoPunch has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. No location data or work-hour data is collected by, sent to, or accessible to the developer or any third party.

Data leaves your device only if you choose to use the Export feature, if you back up (see "Backups" below), or through your own Android device backup if you have that enabled.

If you use the optional Search by address feature, the address you type is sent to your device's built-in location service (on most phones, provided by Google) to convert it into coordinates. It is not sent to the developer.

AutoPunch relies on Google Play services on your device to determine location. Google's handling of location on your device is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

BACKUPS

If you choose to back up, AutoPunch copies your workplaces, shifts and settings either to a folder called "AutoPunch backups" in your own Google Drive, or to a file in a place you choose (for example your phone's Downloads folder or a USB drive). This happens only when you tap "Back up now" or "Save a backup file", or on the schedule you choose if you turn on automatic backup.

  • For Google Drive, AutoPunch asks for permission to see and manage only the files it creates itself (Google's "drive.file" access). It cannot see your other files.
  • Backups go only to your own Google Drive or to the file you choose. They are never sent to the developer, and the developer cannot see them.
  • Backup files you save can be protected with a password (AES-256 encryption). If you forget it, the file cannot be restored.
  • Backups do not include your trial or purchase status or other device-only details.
  • You can stop using Google Drive at any time in Settings > Backup. Backups already made stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Google's handling of your Google Drive is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

FREE TRIAL AND PURCHASE

AutoPunch is free to use for 7 days. After that, a one-time in-app purchase unlocks automatic tracking. The date your trial started and whether the app is unlocked are stored on your device.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer never receives your card or other payment details. Google's handling of purchases is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

YOUR CHOICES

  • Delete any individual work session in the app.
  • Withdraw location permission at any time in Android settings; automatic tracking then stops.
  • Remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Delete backups from the "AutoPunch backups" folder in your Google Drive, or delete backup files you saved.

CHILDREN

AutoPunch is not directed at children under 13.
